SISTERS filename: Sisters Home Page description: A true story of five women attempting to thrive in 19th Century America when women's rights were limited. **************** *Sisters* is a true story of five women to whom my husband is related: Sarah Ann Higby Brand and her younger sister, Clarinda Higby Yourdon; Sarah Etta Brand and Julia Brand, two daughters of Sarah Ann; and Grace Brand Herrick, the daughter of Sarah Etta Brand. It takes place in the 19th century in three places: the Tug Hill plateau of northern New York, the farmlands of Minnesota, and the bustling and busy metropolis of Kansas City, Missouri. Each woman contended with the rules and laws which attempted to control her behaviour.
